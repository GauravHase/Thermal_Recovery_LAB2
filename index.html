Operating parameters

Change any value to update the results and physical explanation automatically. Units are shown beside each field; use Recalculate if you want to refresh manually.

Case-study inputs: Hot drain water 80°C at 15 kg/min (= 0.25 kg/s), incoming cold water 15°C, 24 h/day and 365 days/year, furnace efficiency 80%, natural gas $0.40/therm, and 1 therm = 105,500 kJ. The stated 75% hot-side heat recovery is matched by the default UA of about 3,135 W/K only for the default equal hot/cold heat-capacity rates. The problem does not specify cold-water flow or exchanger UA; 0.25 kg/s cold flow is an explicit modelling assumption, not given data. Constant specific heats are assumed; pressure drop, ambient losses, detailed geometry and capital cost are not modelled.
